About TechBridge

Bridging the gap between talent and opportunity

TechBridge is a tech internship training initiative designed to equip young graduates and career-switchers with in-demand digital skills. We combine structured curricula, live classes, practical assignments and community learning to turn beginners into employable tech professionals.

Our mission is simple: no one should be left behind in the digital economy. Whether you dream of analysing data that drives business decisions or building websites used by thousands, TechBridge gives you the bridge to get there.

    Benefits in TechBridge
  • 30Days intensive, internship-style training
  • Choose between Data Analytics and Web Development
  • Certification plus Recommendation letter on completion
  • Lifetime access to the TechBridge alumni community
  • Fast job alert from the techbridge job board
